Payment Telemetry Architecture Notice

In accordance with the backend security design in PaymentsController.cs, payment transactions are securely tied to individual customer bookings:

  • Total Financial Revenue: Aggregate gross revenue is continuously computed and available via the GET /api/admin/dashboard endpoint.
  • Booking Payment Statuses: Individual transaction references and settlement statuses are tracked on a per-event basis via GET /api/bookings?eventId={id}.
  • Cross-Customer Payment Querying: An unfiltered global payment endpoint is intentionally not exposed on the backend to preserve customer privacy.
